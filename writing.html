My collection

Aside from writing for the games I've made, I also have a collection of other written works - both one-shots and longer pieces - that I've made in my spare time.

Whenever I read a story that I really like, I always find myself thinking of what I would do differently and what might've been interesting directions. In the end, I always end up constructing a new world where I can explore these ideas.

I pride myself on witty dialogue that flows, while preserving the character voice. I love writing dialogue so much, in fact, that I even once tried writing a mystery with almost no descriptions - where it's up to the reader to piece together even who is speaking most of the time.
