About me

My name is Sylvan, a game developer specialising in design and programming. I fell in love with the field a little over a year ago when I started the Game Development & Simulation minor at de Haagse Hogeschool and have done little else since.

I've now chosen to graduate with a major in Game Development & Simulation and am currently in my final year.

Our booth at the expo

My skillset

I've been studying computer science for almost 4 years, and in that time I've gained familiarity with many different languages, whether it's C#, Java, Python, C++.

Since picking up game development, I've been working mainly in GDScript, but I've worked with C# in Unity and am currently working in Unreal with C++. I have a personal philosophy that you only need to understand the flow of programming, so I'm never afraid to try out a new language. It's just a matter of picking up syntax.

While I'm mainly a programmer by trade, I also love writing for and designing games. I simply cannot let poor writing and design slip into a game under my watch, and it's something I often cannot stop thinking about while making games - even in my free time.
